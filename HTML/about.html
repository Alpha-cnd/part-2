Team Bios

Thandi Nkosi Founder & Head Baker

Thandi founded The Golden Crust in 2019 after training under master bakers in Lyon, France, and completing a diploma in Patisserie at the Prue Leith Chefs Academy. Her philosophy is simple: exceptional ingredients, treated with patience and respect, produce exceptional bread. Thandi personally oversees every sourdough batch and develops all new recipes. She is a regular contributor to Taste magazine and was named one of Pretoria's "Top 10 Food Entrepreneurs" in 2024. When not in the bakery, she mentors young women through a local culinary skills programme.

Pieter van der Merwe Pastry Chef

Pieter joined The Golden Crust in 2021, bringing 8 years of experience from boutique hotels in the Cape Winelands. He is responsible for all laminated pastries, choux, and viennoiserie. Pieter is obsessed with temperature control he measures kitchen humidity daily and adjusts proofing times accordingly. His croissants have been described by Eat Out magazine as "the best outside of Paris. " Pieter is also our in-house coffee roaster, ensuring that every pastry has the perfect espresso pairing.

Nomsa Dlamini Front of House & Wholesale Manager

Nomsa is the friendly face behind the counter and the organised mind behind our wholesale operations. She manages relationships with 12 local cafés and restaurants, coordinating daily deliveries and custom orders. Nomsa has a diploma in Hospitality Management and previously worked in guest relations at a five star safari lodge. She knows every regular customer by name and remembers their usual order. Her warmth and efficiency set the tone for the entire Golden Crust experience.
