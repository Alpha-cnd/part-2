The Golden Crust Wholesale Programme


We supply artisan breads and pastries to cafés, restaurants, guest houses, and corporate canteens across Pretoria and Johannesburg. Our wholesale clients receive:


Fresh daily delivery (Monday-Saturday, 06:00-08:00)


Custom product development (seasonal specials, branded items)


Flexible minimum orders (from R500 per delivery)


30 day payment terms for approved accounts


Our current wholesale range includes all sourdough loaves, butter croissants, pain au chocolat, and seasonal muffins. Custom cake orders for hospitality events are available with 14 days' notice. To set up a wholesale account, please complete the enquiry form on this page or email wholesale@thegoldencrust.co.za.
